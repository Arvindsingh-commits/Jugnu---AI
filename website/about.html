About Jugnu AI — आपका अपना देसी AI साथी

Jugnu AI is an Indian artificial intelligence assistant built by Arvind Singh to make smart AI tools accessible in Hindi, Hinglish, English, and regional styles like Rajasthani/Marwari—completely free of cost.

Top Features of Jugnu AI

1. Multilingual & Voice AI Assistant

Ask questions by typing or speaking directly into your microphone. Jugnu AI understands natural Indian English, Hindi, and regional tones to deliver clear, helpful answers.

2. Free AI Image Generator

Turn your imagination into high-resolution artwork, posters, and social media visuals in seconds using text prompts.

3. Smart Study & Homework Helper

Upload photos of textbook questions, handwritten notes, or PDF documents to get step-by-step explanations for school and college subjects.

Best Prompts to Try on Jugnu AI

  • For Students: "Explain Newton's laws of motion in simple Hindi with daily life examples."
  • For Coding: "Write a clean Python program for a calculator and explain every line."
  • For Image Art: "Generate a royal golden tiger in a futuristic Indian palace, cinematic lighting."
